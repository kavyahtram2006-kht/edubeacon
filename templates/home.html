About EduBeacon

EduBeacon is an intelligent priority-based academic alert system developed to improve communication between students, class representatives, and faculty. The system provides a centralized platform for academic announcements, assignments, examination schedules, and study materials. By organizing information based on priority, EduBeacon helps students stay updated, manage their academic responsibilities, and avoid missing important deadlines.
